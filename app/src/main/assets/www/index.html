WORKFLOWS

⚡ Street beat the core loop — 2 minutes from sound to music

hold ● (any page)→walk & record→tap ● to stop→SLICE → PADS→play the city
  1. Hold ● (top bar, any page) — field recording starts instantly, up to 6 min. Walk. A horn, a vendor, rain on tin — collect it all in one take.
  2. Tap ● to stop, then hit SLICE → PADS on the bar that appears: the recording is auto-chopped at sound events and lands across the pads, ready to play.
  3. Go to SEQ, tap ● REC then ▶ — after a 1-bar count-in, drum your beat on the pads. It loops and layers each pass.
  4. Hold FX buttons and drag the pad to perform; ☰ → Export loop when it bangs.

Want finer cuts first? Open CHOP: tap the wave to audition a slice, drag markers, AUTO-SLICE sensitivity, GRID for equal cuts — then → PADS.

🗺 Dig the Sound Map 259+ Saigon field recordings as a crate

☰ or CHOP → 🗺 MAP→search / category / 🎲→▶ preview→→ CHOP or → PAD
  1. The whole Saigon Sound Map archive is your sample library — street vendors, waterways, rituals, markets — searchable by word or category, 🎲 shuffles for surprise.
  2. → CHOP drops a recording into the chop lab (slice it like your own walk); → PAD loads it straight onto the next empty pad — load several in a row.
  3. Needs a connection to browse and load; once a sound is on a pad it's yours offline, saved with the project.

🥁 Voice drums no city needed — beatbox a kit

tap ●→tap pad A1, say "puh"→A2 "tss", A3 "kah"→EDIT: trim + pitch→SEQ
  1. Tap ● once to arm, then tap a pad — it records from the mic until you tap it again. One pad per drum sound.
  2. In EDIT: drag the green/orange handles to trim tight, drop pitch −5 for weight, NORMALIZE for punch.
  3. Put hi-hats in a CHOKE group so closed cuts open, like a real kit.

🎹 One sound → melody a motorbike horn becomes a synth line

pick a pad→EDIT: KEYBOARD MODE→choose a scale→pads = notes→SEQ ● REC
  1. Long-press the pad with your sound → EDIT opens on it.
  2. Turn on KEYBOARD MODE, pick pentatonic minor (hard to hit a wrong note). The 16 pads now play that sound as notes, low at bottom-left.
  3. Record it in SEQ like a beat — the melody keeps its pitches, and exports with them.

Related: 16 LEVELS on the PADS page — one pad spread over 16 velocity steps or 16 semitones (−12…+3). Tap it again to cycle modes.

🌫 Ambient bed the whole walk as texture

CHOP: FULL → PAD→EDIT: LOOP mode→gain low, filter down→FX: REVERB
  1. In CHOP, FULL → PAD puts the entire recording on one pad. Set it to LOOP in EDIT (tap the pad to start/stop it).
  2. Pull filter down to ~800 and gain to ~50% — it becomes a bed under your beat.
  3. Hold REVERB on the FX page and drag right for wash. LATCH keeps it on.

🔁 The resample trick bounce FX into a new sound, then flip it

play something→☰ Resample→perform FX live→STOP → new pad→chop / pitch / reverse it

Resample records what you hear — beat + FX moves — onto the next empty pad. Reverse it, pitch it −12, slice it again. This loop of flipping your own output is where sounds stop resembling their source.

🎛 Playing live performance tools

  1. NOTE RPT + hold a pad = machine-gun rolls on the TC grid (set TC on SEQ; swing applies).
  2. MIX page: mute/solo pads while the loop runs — drop the beat out, bring it back.
  3. ERASE + hold a pad while playing strips its notes as they pass.
  4. Build sequences S1–S8, chain them in ☰ SONG (tap twice = ×2 repeats), then ▶ Play song.
  5. DUP×2 doubles a sequence — record 1 bar, DUP to 4, keep layering variation.

💡 Feel tips

  1. Swing 62–66% at TC 1/16 is the classic head-nod. 75% is full triplet shuffle.
  2. TC OFF records your exact timing — human, loose. Quantize is a choice, not a default.
  3. Soft hits matter: turn FULL LVL off and play quiet hats high on the pad, accents low.
  4. VEL→FILT on a pad makes soft hits darker — instant dynamics.
  5. Everything autosaves. ☰ Save project with a name before you start something new.
